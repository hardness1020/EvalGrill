# Domain rules for Lantern reports

Provided by the (fictional) evidence-review team. Rules are stated the way the
team wrote them — some are mechanically checkable, some are judgment calls, and
one is deliberately vague; part of EvalGrill's job is to notice which is which.

1. **Never cite a source outside the provided packet.** A report that cites or
   relies on a study, article, or expert that is not in the packet is
   unacceptable regardless of anything else it does well.
2. **Every quantitative claim must identify at least one provided source that
   supports it.** Numbers without a packet citation don't count as evidence.
3. **Conflicts between sources must be stated explicitly.** If provided sources
   materially disagree, the report must say so and must not silently merge them
   into one conclusion.
4. **Conclusions must state confidence calibrated to the evidence.** Strong
   consistent evidence warrants a firm conclusion; thin, conflicting, or
   corrected evidence warrants hedged language — in both directions
   (over-hedging strong evidence is also miscalibration).
5. **Reports must be clear and well-organized.**
