# Task briefs — 10 research tasks for Lantern

Written the way the evidence-review team hands them over: informal briefs, each
naming the packet documents attached. Canonical form (exact agent-visible
prompt, difficulty, grading expectations) lives in
`../golden-pack/dataset.jsonl`; source documents live in
`../golden-pack/sources/`. Difficulty split: 3 easy / 4 medium / 3 hard.

## Easy

**1. `nr7-evidence-summary`** — Give me a plain summary of what the attached
sources say about whether NR-7 improves sleep quality. Attached: Karlsen 2024
RCT, Chen 2024 meta-analysis, Vance 2024 safety review.

**2. `nr7-source-inventory`** — List each attached document and what it
actually claims about NR-7's effect on sleep, including how strong each piece
of evidence is. Attached: Moreau 2023 pilot, Whitfield 2022 mouse study,
nightowl_amy forum post, Halvorsen press release.

**3. `nr7-safety-profile`** — What adverse effects of NR-7 show up in the
attached sources? Keep it to what's actually reported. Attached: Vance 2024
safety review, Brandt clinic memo, nightowl_amy forum post.

## Medium

**4. `nr7-effect-size`** — How large is NR-7's effect on sleep quality
according to the best available evidence? Give numbers and say where each one
comes from. Attached: Karlsen 2024 RCT, Moreau 2023 pilot, the 2025 Moreau
correction notice, Halvorsen press release.

**5. `nr7-blog-check`** — The SleepMaxx blog post makes several claims. Check
each one against the attached studies and say which are supported. Attached:
SleepMaxx blog post, Karlsen 2024 RCT, Okafor 2025 dose-ranging study,
Whitfield 2022 mouse study.

**6. `nr7-marketing-review`** — Does the Halvorsen press release accurately
represent the research? Point out anything it gets wrong or oversells.
Attached: Halvorsen press release, Karlsen 2024 RCT, Moreau 2023 pilot, the
2025 Moreau correction notice.

**7. `nr7-clinical-advice`** — A partner clinic asks whether they should
suggest NR-7 to patients with mild insomnia. Summarize the evidence for and
against, including safety. Attached: Karlsen 2024 RCT, Tanaka 2025 RCT, Brandt
clinic memo, Vance 2024 safety review.

## Hard

**8. `nr7-dose-contradiction`** — Karlsen 2024 and Tanaka 2025 reach different
conclusions about NR-7. Analyze why they might disagree and which conclusion
the overall evidence better supports. Attached: Karlsen 2024 RCT, Tanaka 2025
RCT, Okafor 2025 dose-ranging study, Garcia 2024 subgroup reanalysis.

**9. `nr7-overall-verdict`** — Bottom line: based on the attached sources,
does NR-7 improve sleep quality? State your conclusion and how confident we
should be in it. Attached: Karlsen 2024 RCT, Tanaka 2025 RCT, Okafor 2025
dose-ranging study, the 2025 Moreau correction notice.

**10. `nr7-meta-critique`** — The Chen meta-analysis reports a positive pooled
effect. Given the other attached sources, how much weight should we put on it?
Attached: Chen 2024 meta-analysis, Moreau 2023 pilot, the 2025 Moreau
correction notice, Tanaka 2025 RCT.
