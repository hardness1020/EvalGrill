# Moreau et al. (2023) — Open-label pilot study of NR-7 in adults with poor sleep

*Annales du Sommeil*, vol. 17(4), pp. 301–309. Lead author: Dr. Lucie Moreau,
Institut Rives-Claires. **Note: a correction to this article was published in
2025** (see the separate correction notice, if provided in your packet).

## Abstract

**Background.** NR-7 is a commercially available supplement with a proposed
mechanism involving adenosine signalling. Preclinical results have been
encouraging, but human data are lacking. We conducted an exploratory
open-label pilot to estimate the plausible effect range and inform the design
of a controlled trial.

**Methods.** Forty-eight adults (age 25–61) who self-identified as poor
sleepers took NR-7 600 mg nightly for 4 weeks. There was no placebo arm and no
blinding. The outcome was the change in a 10-item self-reported sleep-quality
questionnaire administered at baseline and week 4, expressed as percentage
improvement in total score. Participants were recruited through a university
mailing list and were not compensated.

**Results.** Forty-four participants completed the study. Mean self-reported
sleep-quality scores improved by 31% from baseline (p = 0.01). Thirty-one of
44 completers (70%) reported feeling their sleep had improved "moderately" or
"greatly." Three participants reported unusually vivid dreams; none withdrew
for adverse effects.

**Conclusions.** In this small uncontrolled pilot, 4 weeks of NR-7 was
associated with a large improvement in self-reported sleep quality. These
results must be interpreted with caution: with no placebo arm, expectation
effects and regression to the mean cannot be excluded, and self-report
measures are especially susceptible to both. The study was designed to
estimate feasibility and effect range, not to establish efficacy. We recommend
a double-blind, placebo-controlled trial before any clinical conclusions are
drawn.

## Declared limitations

Open-label design; small sample; self-report outcome; single site; 4-week
duration. The authors declare no financial relationship with Halvorsen Bio;
NR-7 capsules were purchased at retail.
