# PRESS RELEASE — Halvorsen Bio

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — 14 October 2024**

## Clinically proven: Halvorsen Bio's NR-7 improves sleep quality by over 30%

OSLO — Halvorsen Bio, the maker of the premium sleep supplement **NR-7**,
today announced that a growing body of clinical research confirms what
hundreds of thousands of customers already know: NR-7 delivers better sleep.

In a peer-reviewed clinical study published in *Annales du Sommeil*, adults
taking NR-7 nightly experienced a **31% improvement in sleep quality in just
four weeks**. Seven out of ten participants reported that their sleep improved
moderately or greatly. "Numbers like these are simply not seen with ordinary
supplements," said **Marta Voss, Chief Executive Officer of Halvorsen Bio**.
"NR-7 is the most significant advance in sleep science in a decade."

The findings were confirmed this year by the largest NR-7 study ever
conducted: a randomized, placebo-controlled trial of more than 1,200 adults,
published in the *Nordisk Journal of Sleep Research*, which found that NR-7
significantly improved sleep quality compared with placebo (p = 0.003).
Laboratory research has further shown that NR-7 increased deep sleep by more
than 20% and helped subjects fall asleep 27% faster.

"Halvorsen Bio has always believed in rigorous science," added Voss. "With
clinical proof now in hand, our focus is bringing restorative sleep to every
household."

NR-7 is available in 300 mg and 600 mg strengths at leading retailers and at
halvorsenbio.example. As a dietary supplement, NR-7 does not require a
prescription. Individuals with medical conditions should consult a healthcare
professional.
