# Correction notice (2025) — Moreau et al. (2023), open-label pilot of NR-7

*Annales du Sommeil*, vol. 19(1), p. 88. Issued by the editors with the
agreement of all original authors.

## Correction

The editors were contacted by the authors of "Open-label pilot study of NR-7
in adults with poor sleep" (*Annales du Sommeil* 17(4):301–309, 2023) after an
internal re-audit of the study database identified a transcription error
affecting the primary outcome.

During data entry, week-4 questionnaire totals for 11 of the 44 completing
participants were recorded from the wrong questionnaire page, substituting a
subscale score for the total score. The error inflated the apparent
improvement for those participants. The affected records have been re-entered
from the original paper questionnaires, which were retained in full.

## Corrected results

With the corrected data, mean self-reported sleep-quality scores improved by
**12%** from baseline, not 31% as originally reported. The corrected result is
**not statistically significant** (p = 0.09). The proportion of completers
reporting "moderate" or "great" subjective improvement is revised from 70% to
55% (24 of 44). The reported adverse-event observations are unaffected.

The authors state that the corrected findings "should be interpreted as
exploratory only, and should not be cited as evidence that NR-7 improves
sleep quality." They note that the study's original conclusion — that a
double-blind, placebo-controlled trial is required before any clinical
conclusions are drawn — is unchanged and, if anything, reinforced.

## Editorial note

The editors thank the authors for reporting the error proactively. Readers,
reviewers, and authors of secondary literature are asked to cite this
correction alongside the original article. Aggregate analyses (including
published meta-analyses) that incorporated the uncorrected +31% figure should
be interpreted accordingly; the editors are aware of at least one published
pooled analysis that predates this correction and therefore reflects the
inflated value.

The original article remains available with a prominent link to this notice.
No findings other than those listed above are affected, and the editors found
no evidence of intentional misconduct.
